It imports, connects, joins, and unifies all of a company's marketing data — advertising channels, web/app analytics, attribution/measurement, and backend/warehouse data — down to the most granular level, so marketing and data teams can go from question to insight in minutes without SQL, spreadsheets, or waiting on data support. Trusted by enterprises including HelloFresh, Delivery Hero, Bitpanda, and On.

## Marketing Data MCP

- [Marketing Data MCP](https://clarisights.com/marketing-data-mcp): A Model Context Protocol (MCP) server that connects Clarisights to any MCP-compatible AI tool (Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, Gemini). The AI gets six tools — list_data_sources, list_metrics, list_dimensions (discover); query_data (query); list_reports, get_report (navigate) — to explore data, query metrics, and read reports. Queries run against one governed semantic layer with 50+ connectors joined at deepest granularity, expressed in the team's own KPI definitions (blended CAC, contribution-margin ROAS, MER, target-CPI), and respect the same ACL as the logged-in user. Read-only; queries typically span two years of history. Setup: admin enables AI access in Workspace Settings, user retrieves a connection URL from MCP settings, then adds it to their AI tool and signs in with Clarisights credentials.

## Channel Deep Dives

- [Paid Search](https://clarisights.com/use-cases/deep-dive-paid-search): For paid search managers — blend Google Ads, Bing, and Apple Search Ads with attribution and internal data (no scripts or manual Excel). Slice by campaign type (Search, Shopping, PMax), device, and match type; cluster keywords by quality score, ad relevance, landing-page experience, and expected CTR; and view SEM and SEO together to spot cannibalization.
- [Paid Social](https://clarisights.com/use-cases/deep-dive-paid-social): For paid social managers — one consolidated view of all paid social channels with attribution and measurement data joined. Slice by feed vs. reels, audience, and creative concept; uncover creative-level insights and ad fatigue; and stand up best-in-class reports for a new channel (Pinterest, Reddit, X) in ~2 days.
- [Affiliate & Influencer](https://clarisights.com/use-cases/deep-dive-affiliate-influencer): For affiliate/influencer managers — daily visibility across all affiliates, influencers, and partners with attribution, measurement, and first-party data. Publisher-level funnel breakdowns, proactive cost/pacing tracking, 100% cost coverage of niche affiliates and price-comparison sites (via email/CSV/TXT), and support for varied payout structures.
- [App Acquisition](https://clarisights.com/use-cases/deep-dive-app-acquisition): For UA managers — MMP, attribution, and internal data joined at the most granular level across apps, networks, cohorts, and countries. Go beyond CPI with full-funnel, retention, revenue, and pLTV metrics; find creative-level insights; and add ASO/app-store reporting (organic vs. paid installs, rankings, keywords).
- [SEO](https://clarisights.com/use-cases/deep-dive-seo): For SEO managers — Search Console and analytics data blended daily across all domains. Break down by country, brand vs. non-brand, and page type down to the query level, estimate conversions by applying SEA conversion rates to organic traffic, prevent SEM/SEO cannibalization, and import backlink and rank data from external SEO tools.
- [CRM](https://clarisights.com/use-cases/deep-dive-crm): For CRM managers — CRM, MMP, attribution, and internal (revenue, targets) data joined at the most granular level. Slice by communication channel (email, push, in-app), audience, and campaign type; track trends and set alerts; and view acquisition and retention together to see which messaging and offers work best.
- [Display & Retargeting](https://clarisights.com/use-cases/deep-dive-display-retargeting): For display/remarketing managers — channel, measurement, attribution, and internal data blended in one view. Slice by new vs. existing customer, audience, and creative concept; uncover creative-level insights and fatigue; and compare display's upper-funnel and retargeting contribution against paid social, Google Ads, and other channels.
